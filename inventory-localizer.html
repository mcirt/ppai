v0.13.24 geometry rule

Three-window geometry is tried first at a 66% lock threshold, with a hard uniform-size gate. If that does not lock, two trustworthy dark windows can establish the inventory scale and side length; the missing position is inferred from the measured shallow-∧ geometry (Y sits above the X/Z baseline) rather than projected along a straight line or filled by an unrelated speck. The two-circle path locks at 64% and can locally refine the predicted position only with a nearby component of matching size. The approved tight silhouette is attempted only after those circle-based paths fail. The adaptive bright-paint emoji classifier is unchanged in this build.
