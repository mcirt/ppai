PPAI JS v0.13.7 — V0.13.5 LOCALIZATION + VIEWFINDER CAPTURE + READINGS

Important:
This build intentionally starts from v0.13.5.

Localization behavior / thresholds from v0.13.5 are preserved.
The v0.13.6 three-circle hard-gate changes are NOT carried forward.

Only two functional additions are layered onto the v0.13.5 base:

1. EXACT VIEWFINDER CAPTURE
   Take Photo now reproduces the short viewfinder's CSS object-fit: cover crop.
   Hidden pyramid content outside the visible viewfinder is no longer included.

2. RECOGNITION RESULTS
   After the unchanged v0.13.5 localizer produces normalized X/Y/Z crops,
   each crop is compared against images/emoji1.png through emoji18.png.
   The standalone page displays:
   - E number
   - color family
   - confidence
   - runner-up
   - best-vs-second margin

Recognition is review-only.
Nothing is written into index.html tile data or the solver.
