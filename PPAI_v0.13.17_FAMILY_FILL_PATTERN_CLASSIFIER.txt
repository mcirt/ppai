WHAT CHANGED
------------
The weak fuzzy sibling selector has been replaced.

Recognition now works in three stages:

1) COLOR FAMILY
   red / orange / yellow / green / blue / purple

2) COLORED-FILL MAP
   Inside the detected emoji disk, the scanner measures where the brighter
   family-colored pixels live. Dark complementary portions receive much less weight.

3) SIBLING GEOMETRY
   Only the three siblings in that color family are compared:
   - partial A
   - partial B
   - full

The comparison uses:
- overall colored coverage (partial vs full)
- colored centroid (which side/half)
- 8 angular sectors
- 4 quadrants
- 4x4 spatial fill grid

X and Y use the same known occlusion mask on the live crop AND on every reference,
so the plaque in front should not make a full emoji falsely appear partial.

WHY
---
v0.13.16 correctly identified colors but repeatedly biased toward the full-color
third sibling (for example E15 blue and E6 orange). v0.13.17 explicitly solves
the partial-A / partial-B / full distinction instead of asking generic image
similarity to infer it.
