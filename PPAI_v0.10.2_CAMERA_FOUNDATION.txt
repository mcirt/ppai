PPAI JS v0.10.2 — Camera / OpenCV Foundation

PURPOSE
-------
This is deliberately the first scanner milestone only.

Finished user workflow remains:
1. Scan Pyramid -> Tiles 1–28
2. Scan Inventory in 8 groups of 3 -> Tiles 29–52

v0.10.2 does NOT recognize or populate tiles yet.

WHAT WORKS IN THIS BUILD
------------------------
- A Scan Pyramid button lives in index.html.
- It opens the rear camera using the same getUserMedia pattern proven in the FreeCell project.
- The entire pyramid only needs to be reasonably visible inside a loose guide.
- Capture produces a still image in a canvas.
- The exact known-good local FreeCell OpenCV build is included at js/opencv.js.
- The OpenCV loader uses the proven custom cv.then(callback) + 100 ms Safari polling pattern.
- It does NOT use `await cv` or resolve a Promise with cv itself.
- After capture, OpenCV performs grayscale + Canny edge detection as a proof that the captured camera pixels reached OpenCV successfully.
- The captured JPEG is cached in sessionStorage as ppaiLastPyramidCapture for the next geometry milestone.
- game.html and solver.html are the working PPAI v0.10.1 files and are not strategically changed.
