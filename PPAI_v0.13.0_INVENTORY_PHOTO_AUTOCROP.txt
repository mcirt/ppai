PPAI JS v0.13.0 — INVENTORY PHOTO AUTO-CROP

PIVOT
-----
The 3-circle live alignment template is removed.

New workflow:
1. Tiles 1–28 are already filled manually.
2. Tap Scan Inventory Photo.
3. Frame the three visible inventory cards naturally.
4. Tap Capture 3 Inventory Tiles.
5. OpenCV automatically finds the three circular emoji emblems.
6. The three circles are sorted left-to-right as X / Y / Z.
7. The scanner crops and recognizes each emoji.
8. Results are shown for human review.
9. Accept is enabled after every successful recognition, even when confidence is low.
10. Continue with 32–34, 35–37, ... 50–52.

Canvas / JavaScript:

AUTOMATIC 3-CIRCLE GROUPING
---------------------------
The detector filters compact, near-square/circular contour candidates and then
scores groups of three for:
- similar radius
- similar vertical level
- reasonable horizontal spacing
- balanced X/Y and Y/Z gaps
- central placement in the captured photo

The winning group is sorted left-to-right:
left   = X
middle = Y
right  = Z

OCCLUSION
---------
X and Y still use slot-specific soft right-side masks because they are partially
covered in the real game.
Z uses the full crop.

ACCEPT BEHAVIOR
---------------
Accept is no longer blocked by confidence/stability.

After recognition succeeds, Accept is always clickable.
Low confidence is shown as a warning only.

The human review is the final gate.
